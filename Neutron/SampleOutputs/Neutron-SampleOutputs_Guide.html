Figure 1 — Beat 0: the default model's pressure profile

python main.py

Pressure falls smoothly and monotonically from the center to zero at the program's estimated surface radius, 7.804 km.

What it shows

  • The plot's default output_type, pressure against radius, for the program's default model (gamma=1.666667, pC=1.26×1035 Pa, K=5.3802×103, steps_per_scale=400).
  • A smooth, monotonic decline from the central pressure to exactly zero at the plotted curve's right-hand endpoint.
  • That zero endpoint is set by construction at the program's estimated surface radius, not found by locating an exact zero of a continuous function; Beat 5 discusses how close the estimate is to the star's true zero-pressure radius.

Headline values

  • Surface radius 7.804 km; total mass 0.962591 Msun; 479 radial samples (nominal dr=16.375 m).
  • Central pressure 1.26×1035 Pa; central density 6.633451×1018 kg/m3; central sound speed vs/c=0.593498.
  • Compactness 2GM/(Rc2)=0.364302; causality check satisfied; Buchdahl check satisfied.

Figure 2 — Beat 4: enclosed mass at the default resolution

python main.py --steps_per_scale 400 --output_type mass

Enclosed mass grows monotonically from zero at the center to 0.962591 solar masses at the surface, for the same model shown in Figure 1.

What it shows

  • The mass profile of the default model, at the default radial resolution (steps_per_scale=400) used in Beat 4's convergence check.
  • A smooth, monotonic rise from zero at the center to the model's total mass at the surface.
  • The curve's shape is essentially unchanged across the resolutions Beat 4 compares; refining the grid moves the endpoint values only in digits far below plot resolution.

Headline values

  • At steps_per_scale=400 (shown): surface radius 7.803563 km, total mass 0.96259065 Msun, 479 samples.
  • At 200: 7.803970 km, 0.96259066 Msun, 240 samples. At 800: 7.803543 km, 0.96259064 Msun, 955 samples. At 1600: 7.803139 km, 0.96259064 Msun, 1908 samples.
  • Total mass is already stable to eight figures at the default resolution; surface radius keeps drifting, but only in the fourth decimal of a kilometre.

Figure 3 — Beat 6: a causality-violating high-pressure model

python main.py --pC 1e37 --output_type mass

At a central pressure of 1037 Pa the model's central sound speed exceeds c, so the causality check fails even though the Buchdahl bound remains comfortably satisfied.

What it shows

  • The mass profile of a much higher central-pressure model than the default, from Beat 6's four-run comparison.
  • A smaller, more compact star than Figures 1–2, with a correspondingly steeper approach to its (lower) total mass.
  • A model whose printed causality check fails while its Buchdahl check still passes — the two diagnostics are independent, and one can fail well before the other.

Headline values

  • At pC=1037 Pa (shown): surface radius 4.122 km, total mass 0.650649 Msun, compactness 0.466203, central sound speed vs/c=1.423460 — causality check NOT satisfied; Buchdahl check satisfied.
  • At pC=1034, 1035 and 1036 Pa: compactness 0.203957, 0.349845 and 0.462172; central sound speed vs/c=0.357557, 0.566689 and 0.898142; all three causality and Buchdahl checks satisfied.
  • None of the four runs approaches Buchdahl's bound of 8/9≈0.8889, even though the last one fails causality.

Figure 4 — Beat 7: the low-mass end of the sequence

python main.py --pC 1e28 --output_type mass

At a central pressure of 1028 Pa the model is far larger and far less massive than the default star, illustrating the low-density end of Beat 7's mass turnover.

What it shows

  • The mass profile of a much lower central-pressure model than the default, from Beat 7's central-pressure sequence.
  • A far larger, far less compact, far less massive star than Figures 1–3.
  • The low-density end of Investigation 20.1's puzzle: a single high-density polytrope extrapolated into a regime a real low-density neutron star would not occupy.

Headline values

  • At pC=1028 Pa (shown): surface radius 54.713 km, total mass 0.020979 Msun, compactness 0.001132, causality check satisfied.
  • Across the sequence's turnover, total mass is 0.818445, 0.963016 and 0.849747 Msun at pC=1034, 1035 and 1036 Pa — rising then falling, with a maximum between 1035 and 1036 Pa.
  • At pC=1027 Pa, total mass falls further, to 0.010529 Msun, below the roughly 0.02-solar-mass threshold flagged as implausibly low for a real neutron star.
  • --max_steps 100 exits with an error (the surface is not reached in 100 steps) instead of printing a summary; --gamma 1 is rejected before integration begins, the same as in Beat 2.

Notes for evaluation

  • All four figures and every headline value above were regenerated from this release; none is carried over from an earlier build.
  • Each figure's command can be run directly from the program folder; a student's output that disagrees with the values above usually points to a changed default or a mistyped flag, not a program defect.
  • Beats 1, 2, 3 and 5 of the Help are demonstrated with a short script or a printed diagnostic rather than a plot, so they have no figure in this guide.
