Major Improvements in This Release

  • --log selects a logarithmic vertical axis for pressure, density or temperature; altitude stays linear. --no-log and omission preserve the linear default. Axis labels and cursor coordinates retain physical units. A log figure identifies its scale in its title.
  • Exercise 2 compares identical isothermal inputs on linear and logarithmic scales, confines the straight-line investigation to 0–32 km, and distinguishes natural-log slope from base-10 decades. Parameters and Output document the options.
  • All four uploaded production modules matched the September 27 Programs snapshot byte for byte before this change. The integrator and checkpoint calculations remain unchanged. All 16 Help tutorial commands retain identical numerical console output (except version/build heading) and byte-identical default linear PNGs in this environment.
  • The merged Help preserves the user's revised short/Grok baseline and Schutz-derived additions. Against September 27 Documentation, the comparison is to Atmosphere-grok.html, renamed through Atmosphere-short.html to Atmosphere.html. Changes made in this maintenance round are confined to version/build, the new option and Exercise 2.
  • The SampleOutputs Guide has eight newly generated figures, including a logarithmic Beat 2 plot, corrected contents links, current metadata and verified headline values. No earlier images were reused.

Known Limitations

  • Constant gravity, constant composition and supplied temperature remain educational model assumptions. First-order Euler errors, restart behavior, step budget and the upper-profile closure are unchanged from 1.6.0.
  • Logarithmic plots require finite positive quantity values; nonpositive points are masked. The numerical top remains the last stored positive-pressure sample, not a physical boundary.
  • A2, an altitude-dependent-gravity coding exercise, is deliberately declined. The existing qualitative gravity investigation remains unchanged.
  • Reference-fixture provenance limitations and MathJax's public-CDN dependency remain as recorded in the retained notes.

Known Limitations

  • Educational constant-gravity, constant-composition ideal-gas model. Temperature is supplied, not computed.
  • First-order Euler: 2.93% low at 100 km for an isothermal 288.15 K atmosphere when the step is not enlarged. After a restart the step is coarser and that few-per-cent figure does not apply. A 0-3000 km isothermal run that restarts is about 83% low and prints no warning that the step was enlarged.
  • The T = beta p^0.5 continuation is a pedagogical closure. The printed Numerical top is a stopping rule and depends on the step.
  • A profile with more positive nodes than MAX_STEPS cannot finish. An extremely steep or finely sampled profile can still exhaust the 50,000-point budget; if every step in that pass was already shorter than the nominal increment the error is raised after one pass.
  • U.S. Standard Atmosphere 1976 layer-base pressures are a secondary-source fixture. Venus values are from a secondary compilation. Mars and Jupiter are round-number illustrations. The seven NRLMSIS temperatures are for one date and place. Those fixtures are consistency checks, not primary-source validation.
  • A checkpoint outside the positive-pressure domain has no model pressure.
  • Help files load MathJax from a public CDN.
  • Public get_temp() scans the whole profile on every call (O(n)).

Known Minor Maintenance Items

  • M_PROTON is absent; a direct importer gets AttributeError.
  • Some test method names still say molecular weight.
  • Long-Help Experiments 6 and 7 still give no numerical check values. Experiment 8 still asks for the surface-temperature step to be restored without quoting an expected result.
  • --save_plot . writes a hidden .png; a path without a suffix is written as PATH.png by Matplotlib.
  • Both Help files still name Chapter 7 in the page header. Whether a Help file should name a book chapter is an open question for Prof. Schutz; the badges are unchanged until he decides.
  • Inline formulas outside the pinned, counted physical-relation list are not covered by tests, including the two Open Bugs above.
