Major Improvements in This Release

Version 1.6.0 (Audit46 response, this package):

  • Each displayed equation in Atmosphere-grok.html (7 blocks) and Atmosphere-claude.html (10 blocks) is pinned as a whole whitespace-stripped math block. Changing any character in those blocks fails the suite.
  • Restored pins: Eq. (7.3) minus sign and factors; both equalities of the surface scale-height definition; both differences and the validity range of the interpolation; the upper-closure exponent alpha = 0.5; the Euler update using rho_{j-1} Delta h, not Delta h^2.
  • Selected inline physical relations are pinned (scale-height definitions, N = 200, gas-law fall factor, barometric and hydrostatic signs).

Version 1.6.0 (Audit45, Audit44 and Audit43, carried forward):

  • Short Help Beat 4 and the Sample Outputs Guide teach the 50-to-100 km fall factors with T(100 km)/T(50 km) = 350/200 = 1.75.
  • Long-Help tests bind to Atmosphere-claude.html whenever that file exists. Displayed Eq. (7.1) must have the minus sign in each named Help.
  • A valid in-place profile edit drops the cached upper-atmosphere coefficient. A fully capped 50,000-point pass raises a budget error after one pass and names a steep or finely sampled profile.
  • The temperature-change cap bounds |Delta T| by 5% of the temperature at the start of the step. Every run prints a Numerical top line.

Known Limitations

  • Educational constant-gravity, constant-composition ideal-gas model. Temperature is supplied, not computed.
  • First-order Euler: 2.93% low at 100 km for an isothermal 288.15 K atmosphere when the step is not enlarged. After a restart the step is coarser and that few-per-cent figure does not apply. A 0-3000 km isothermal run that restarts is about 83% low and prints no warning that the step was enlarged.
  • The T = beta p^0.5 continuation is a pedagogical closure. The printed Numerical top is a stopping rule and depends on the step.
  • A profile with more positive nodes than MAX_STEPS cannot finish. An extremely steep or finely sampled profile can still exhaust the 50,000-point budget; if every step in that pass was already shorter than the nominal increment the error is raised after one pass.
  • U.S. Standard Atmosphere 1976 layer-base pressures are a secondary-source fixture. Venus values are from a secondary compilation. Mars and Jupiter are round-number illustrations. The seven NRLMSIS temperatures are for one date and place. Those fixtures are consistency checks, not primary-source validation.
  • A checkpoint outside the positive-pressure domain has no model pressure.
  • Help files load MathJax from a public CDN.
  • Public get_temp() scans the whole profile on every call (O(n)).

Known Minor Maintenance Items

  • M_PROTON is absent; a direct importer gets AttributeError.
  • Some test method names still say molecular weight.
  • Long-Help Experiments 6 and 7 still give no numerical check values. Experiment 8 still asks for the surface-temperature step to be restored without quoting an expected result.
  • --save_plot . writes a hidden .png; a path without a suffix is written as PATH.png by Matplotlib.
  • Both Help files still name Chapter 7 in the page header. Whether a Help file should name a book chapter is an open question for Prof. Schutz; the badges are unchanged until he decides.
  • Inline formulas that are not in the pinned physical-relation list are not covered by tests.
