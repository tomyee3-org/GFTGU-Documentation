Atmosphere 1.5.0 Release Notes

Version: 1.5.0
Build: d12634057d18
Date: 2026-09-25

Release Status

Version 1.5.0 follows 1.4.0 through two code-changing rounds since those notes: a Grok Kickoff-4 audit of the shared core against both Beats Help files, and the fixes listed below. The 1.4.0 notes recorded three open bugs (OB-1, OB-2, OB-3). This release closes all three. Grok recommends accepting 1.5.0. Independent review by Claude and Codex has not yet reported on this build. These notes replace the 1.4.0 notes; the earlier notes are not reproduced.

Open Bugs

None at P1-P3. The three defects carried from 1.4.0 are closed in this release:

  • OB-1 (was P2, Codex A22-P2-01), closed. Symptom was a silently large Euler error when temperature breakpoints were closer together than the altitude step. The integrator now lands on every supplied temperature breakpoint above the reference level, so a thin hot or cold layer cannot hide between samples. The 100-cell spike profile that was 39% low at 292.56 m is now within 2% of the exact piecewise-linear integral.
  • OB-2 (was P3, Codex A22-P3-01), closed. Symptom was that extract_checkpoints() and extract_output() accepted a physically invalid AtmosphereResult. Both now reject non-positive pressures, densities or temperatures, and an altitude span that overflows interpolation arithmetic, with ValueError.
  • OB-3 (was P3, Codex A22-P3-02), closed. Symptom was that TemperatureProfile.get_temp() raised IndexError or ZeroDivisionError on an empty or non-increasing profile. It now raises ValueError.

Major Improvements in This Release

Version 1.5.0:

  • The Euler integrator lands on every supplied temperature breakpoint above altitude zero, in addition to stepping by Hmin/200. Default-run stored length is 20,940 points (was 20,933). Pressures at 11 km are unchanged to five figures; at 86 km the printed value is 0.30439 Pa (was 0.30438 Pa); the numerical top is 572.441 km (was 572.418 km).
  • extract_output() and extract_checkpoints() reject non-positive pressures, densities and temperatures, and an unusable altitude span.
  • TemperatureProfile.get_temp() raises ValueError for an empty or non-increasing profile instead of IndexError or ZeroDivisionError.
  • New command-line flags --save_plot PATH and --no_show write the figure to a file and skip the plot window. Sample outputs were regenerated with those flags.
  • User-facing wording uses mean molecular mass rather than mean molecular weight. Help files state that the p/T column is density without the factor μmu/kB, that the default thermosphere uses seven NRLMSIS temperatures, and that the gas-law coefficient inherits the rounding already present in μ and mu.
  • Both Beats Help files (Atmosphere-grok.html and Atmosphere-claude.html) share this core, this test suite, and this Sample Outputs guide. The suite recognises either layout. Quoted checkpoint numbers in the long Help were refreshed where five-figure values moved.

Test Suite Growth

177 to 186 automated tests (1.4.0 to 1.5.0). The suite is run once from tests/; all 186 pass under CPython 3.12 in about 36 s with the short Help beside the program (27 long-Help assertions skipped) and in about 46 s with the long Help (4 short-Help assertions skipped). No test was removed.

New coverage: landing on thin temperature layers against the exact piecewise-linear integral; ValueError from get_temp() on an empty or non-increasing profile; rejection of non-positive or unusable AtmosphereResult arrays; --save_plot / --no_show; layout recognition for the short Beats script; smoke checks that the short script does not pre-print the checkpoint table.

Known Limitations

  • Educational constant-gravity, constant-composition ideal-gas model. The temperature profile is supplied, not computed. Not an atmosphere forecast.
  • The first-order Euler method still accumulates error that grows with height (2.93% low at 100 km for an isothermal 288.15 K atmosphere). A profile that exhausts the 50,000-point budget restarts with a larger step.
  • The upper continuation T = βp0.5 is a pedagogical closure. The computed top is a stopping rule, and its altitude depends on the step.
  • The Beat 6 U.S. Standard Atmosphere 1976 layer-base pressures are a test fixture transcribed from a secondary reproduction of the layer table and checked against the standard's defining constants. The NTRS scan of the primary tables is of poor quality and was not used as a digitization source.
  • The Venus temperatures and the two published pressures in Beat 5 come from the Wikipedia article “Atmosphere of Venus,” a secondary compilation, not VIRA. The Mars and Jupiter profiles are round-number illustrations.
  • The seven rounded 100-500 km NRLMSIS 2.0 temperatures are illustrative and specific to 15 October 2024, 12:00 UTC, 55 deg N, 45 deg E. The CCMC run identifier, indices and raw output are not archived.
  • A checkpoint outside the computed positive-pressure domain has no model pressure or p/T; the report says so.
  • Extreme parameter combinations that exceed the floating-point domain are rejected with an error.
  • The Help files load MathJax from a public CDN. Offline, formulas appear as TeX source.

Known Minor Maintenance Items

  • M_PROTON still does not exist in physics_atmosphere; a direct caller importing it gets AttributeError. This remains a 1.x change.
  • A few internal comments and test method names still say “molecular weight” where the user-facing text now says “mean molecular mass.”
  • The short Help’s experiments still ask questions without printed check values. That is the house-style choice for that file, not an omission of a number the code cannot produce.
  • The generated-profile accuracy tests bound the error for their own profile families. Landing on breakpoints closed the previous counter-example; a still more pathological profile that the validator accepts could exist.
