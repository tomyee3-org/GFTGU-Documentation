Release Status

Version 1.5.1 follows 1.5.0 through two code-changing rounds: Grok Kickoff-4 (1.5.0, build d12634057d18), and this Audit41 response. Claude Audit41 and Codex Audit41 both recommended holding 1.5.0 until their P2 items were closed. Those P2 items, and the P3 items listed under Improvements, are addressed here. Grok recommends accepting 1.5.1. Claude and Codex have not yet reviewed this build. These notes replace the 1.5.0 notes; earlier notes are not reproduced.

Open Bugs

None at P1-P3 from Audit41 that remain unresolved in this build.

Major Improvements in This Release

Version 1.5.1 (Audit41 response):

  • Euler steps inside the supplied profile are shortened when temperature would change by more than about 5% in one sample (Codex P2-01). Landing on breakpoints is unchanged. The 300 K / 3000 K sawtooth that was 24% low at 8000 m is now 0.18% low. Default-run stored length, first step, 11 km pressure, 86 km pressure and numerical top are unchanged from 1.5.0.
  • The long Help algorithm, Experiment 9 patch, plotter excerpt and main() tail now describe the 1.5.x loop: step may be shorter than dh, and plotting goes through save_and_maybe_show (Codex P2-02, Claude A41-P2-01, A41-P3-01). Experiment 9 quotes 0.36195 Pa at 86 km from that printed patch.
  • The test suite discovers every active Help file on the search path and checks both even when one of them sits next to main.py (Codex P2-03, Claude A41-P3-04). Short-Help scientific claims are tied to live runs (Claude A41-P2-02).
  • get_temp() validates the profile only when the lists look new, and interpolates with bisect (Claude A41-P3-05).
  • --save_plot rejects an empty path at parse time; a missing directory or unsupported format exits with Atmosphere plot error (Codex P3-03, Claude A41-P3-06).
  • Every run prints a Numerical top line giving the last stored altitude and temperature.

Version 1.5.0 (Kickoff-4), carried forward:

  • Breakpoint landing; rejection of non-positive result arrays; ValueError from an empty or non-increasing profile; --save_plot / --no_show.

Test Suite Growth

186 to 199 automated tests (1.5.0 to 1.5.1). The suite is run from tests/. With both Help files on the search path and the short file next to the program: 199 pass, 27 long-Help-only assertions skipped, in about 43 s. The long Help is still audited by DualHelpFileTests and the named-file loaders. No test was removed. Two assertions that had been loosened for both layouts are again conditional on layout.

Known Limitations

  • Educational constant-gravity, constant-composition ideal-gas model. Temperature is supplied, not computed.
  • First-order Euler: 2.93% low at 100 km for an isothermal 288.15 K atmosphere when the step is not enlarged. After a restart the step is coarser and that few-per-cent figure does not apply.
  • The T = beta p^0.5 continuation is a pedagogical closure. The printed Numerical top is a stopping rule.
  • U.S. Standard Atmosphere 1976 layer-base pressures are a secondary-source fixture. Venus values are from a secondary compilation. Mars and Jupiter are round-number illustrations. The seven NRLMSIS temperatures are for one date and place. Those fixtures are consistency checks, not primary-source validation.
  • A checkpoint outside the positive-pressure domain has no model pressure.
  • Help files load MathJax from a public CDN.

Known Minor Maintenance Items

  • M_PROTON is absent; a direct importer gets AttributeError.
  • Some test method names still say molecular weight.
  • Long-Help Experiments 6 and 7 still give no numerical check values. Experiment 8 still asks for the surface-temperature step to be restored without quoting an expected result.
  • A restart still does not print a warning that the step was enlarged.
  • Help files still mention GFTGU Chapter 7 in the badge and description.
