What the program calculates

RelativisticOrbit integrates equatorial test-particle motion around a one-solar-mass Schwarzschild source, using the particle’s proper time as the independent variable. A Newtonian switch permits controlled comparisons using the same initial diagram coordinates and derivatives.

The displayed x and y values are an orbital diagram constructed from Schwarzschild areal radius and azimuth. They are not global Cartesian coordinates in flat space, and dy/dtau is not a locally measured physical speed.

The supplied strong-field initial data produce a bound rosette. Successive periapsis markers rotate around the central mass instead of returning to one fixed direction.

Notes for evaluation

  • Physical scope: the orbiting body is a test particle in the equatorial plane of a nonrotating one-solar-mass Schwarzschild source. It does not influence the central mass, radiate gravitational waves, or model spin.
  • Numerical method: a constant-acceleration predictor and iterated trapezoidal corrector use adaptive timestep reduction, followed by gradual recovery toward the user’s dt ceiling.
  • Diagnostics: the result records unwrapped azimuth, periapsis locations, mean apsidal advance, termination reason, and maximum fractional drift of specific angular momentum and the effective-energy integral.
  • Reference circles: the solid horizon and dashed ISCO circles are drawn at their true Schwarzschild areal radii. The central point marker is only a fixed-size visibility aid.
  • Interpretation: the Newtonian-looking coordinate equation is an exact rewriting of equatorial timelike Schwarzschild geodesic motion in proper time; its coordinate acceleration is not a freely falling particle’s physical proper acceleration, which is zero.
  • Suggested investigations: the help file progresses from rosette regularity and GR/Newtonian comparison through capture, circular geodesics, convergence, motion inside the ISCO, approach to the photon orbit, zoom–whirl tuning, and weak-field precession.
  • Build verification: the executable and documentation both identify version 1.0.3, build 14ee4ae56c17. In accordance with the preparation instructions, no regression suite was run.
