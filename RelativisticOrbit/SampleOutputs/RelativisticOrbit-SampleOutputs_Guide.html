Figure 4 — Whirl and horizon plunge

python main.py --u_init 1.044e8 --dt 1e-7 --eps1 0.005 --eps2 1e-6 --max_steps 12000

Model boundaries

The source is a fixed nonrotating one-solar-mass mass, without backreaction, gravitational-wave losses, or spin. The x-y plot is constructed from areal radius and azimuth; dy/dτ is a coordinate derivative, not a local measured speed. The Newtonian control is useful for judging whether a small reported apsidal advance could be numerical drift.
